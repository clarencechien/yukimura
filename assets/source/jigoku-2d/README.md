# Illustration source for the 2.5D lighting study

`yukimura-illustration-cutout.png` was prepared once with image_gen on 2026-10-15 from the left/front figure in `docs/routes/jigoku-2d/art/02-yukimura-model-reference.png`. The preparation requested one isolated, complete transparent character in the same relaxed standing pose, without the separate spear or reference panels. It is a generated derivative, not an exact pixel extraction of the earlier sheet.

The subsequent neutral/warm/cool files are Blender light-pass composites sharing this single source. They were not individually regenerated. `relight-surfaces.json` contains manually authored broad relief regions in top-left image coordinates, not model-predicted depth.

All source pixels, including the original alpha, are retained in this PNG. The image's original baked lighting remains part of the final art. See `docs/routes/jigoku-2d/RELIGHT_STUDY.md` for the method and limits.

## Spear motion study

`yukimura-yari-ready.png` (1536×1024 RGBA) was generated once with image_gen on 2026-10-15, referencing `yukimura-illustration-cutout.png`. The request preserved red lacquer, six coins, antlers, menpo, indigo cloth and chainmail; added a straight jumonji-yari with spaced two-handed grip and planted staggered stance; requested a transparent landscape cutout and elevated orthographic view. The actual image is a shallow three-quarter view, not a calibrated 30-degree isometric view. It is a newly generated pose, not a pixel-identical transformation of the original standing art. The generator supplied no reproducible seed. No third-party art was downloaded for this derivative.

`yari-surfaces.json` is a separate manually authored relief for this image. Three light plates were calculated in Blender before animation. The continuous frames in `YARI_MOTION.md` come from Blender shape-key deformation of these same image pixels, not independent image generation. The weapon is baked into the source image; it is not yet an independently swappable layer.

## Duel action key poses

`ghost-rig/duel-bodies.png` (2172×724 RGBA) and `yari-guard-poses.png` (1932×814 RGBA) were generated by two image_gen reference edits on 2026-10-15 UTC. References were the original ghost-warrior guard body and Yukimura warm-left idle frame 0 from commit `d496634`, respectively. They request the existing painterly armor, planted feet and plausible weight shifts. The enemy source omits the weapon-side arm for reuse of the game's original rigid arm, hand and katana. The hero source includes the spear; its projection and hand spacing vary between drawings and are not constrained by the existing weapon rig.

`tools/prepare_jigoku_duel_art.py` extracts sprites through fully transparent gutters, verifies alpha preservation, registers manually marked feet using uniform scale, and packs desktop/mobile atlases. It does not warp anatomy or generate new interpolation frames. `docs/routes/jigoku-2d/combat/duel-art.json` records source hashes, generation filenames, sockets, memory sizes and viewport bounds. No new third-party assets, Blender rendering, or Modal jobs were used for these five key poses.

## Shared spear and guard handoffs

`guard-rig/yari.png` is one image_gen reconstruction of the existing spear without the original hand/cloth remnants. `guard-rig/body-first-study.png` is the first four-pose body edit referencing idle and the previous two guards; its front hands were too low. A targeted image_gen edit produced `guard-rig/bodies.png`, the runtime source with higher front hands. These three calls preserve the painterly armor direction but do not provide pixel-identical geometry or reproducible seeds.

`tools/prepare_guard_rig.py` uniformly registers the soles, crops foreground finger/cuff layers from the same body images and packs one fixed-length spear. It records manually marked palm centers and the reference study in `docs/routes/jigoku-2d/combat/guard-rig.json`. Phaser places the single 760 px spear along the palm axis, using fixed 237 px grip anchors; the front drawn palm is within 3 px of its anchor. The arm/body images are not warped to fit. Idle/thrust/sweep retain their earlier baked weapon; sharing does not yet extend to those clips. No Blender/Modal job or new third-party asset was used for this step.
